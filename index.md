# AstroScanner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

AstroScanner ("the app", package `com.astroscanner.app`) is a document scanner and file vault for Android, published by DigiVault Services ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Contact: digivaultservice@gmail.com

## Summary

- The app works offline. Your documents stay on your phone unless you turn on Google Drive backup.
- We run no servers for this app. We do not receive, store or see your documents or any personal information.
- The app contains no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.

## Information the app handles

**Documents, photos and files.** Scans, imported files, folders, tags and the text recognised from them are stored on your device. Text recognition (OCR) runs on the device. Nothing is uploaded to us.

**Camera.** The camera is used only to scan documents and QR codes. Images are processed on your device.

**App PIN, biometrics and recovery answers.** The PIN, folder PINs and recovery questions are used only to unlock the app on your device. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.

**Google Drive backup (optional).** If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google Account and the app uploads encrypted copies of your files to your own Google Drive. Files are encrypted on your phone before upload, using a recovery key that you hold; we cannot read them and do not hold the key. The app asks only for the `drive.file` permission, which lets it access files it created itself. The files are stored in your Google account and are governed by Google's privacy policy. The app shows the linked account name on screen but does not send it to us.

**Sharing between phones (optional).** You can send files directly to another phone running AstroScanner using a QR code, manual code or NFC. The transfer goes directly between the two devices using Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Google Play services, and requires confirmation on both phones. It does not pass through any server of ours.

**Sharing to other apps.** When you use Android's Share menu, the file goes to the app you choose, under that app's own policy.

**Google Play services.** The QR scanner and nearby sharing use Google Play services, which are provided by Google and may handle data as described in Google's privacy policy.

## Permissions

Camera (scanning), Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and nearby devices (phone-to-phone transfer), NFC (optional pairing), Internet (Drive backup), notifications and a foreground service for data sync (to show backup and restore progress). Location permission on older Android versions is requested only because Android requires it for nearby Bluetooth/Wi-Fi discovery; the app does not collect or use your location.

## Data sharing and retention

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties. We hold none of your data. Data on your device is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Backups in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them in Google Drive.

## Security

Drive backups are encrypted on your device before upload and sent over HTTPS. The app PIN and biometric lock control access to the app but do not encrypt files stored on your phone. Keep your recovery key safe: if you lose it, we cannot recover your backup.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for users aged 18 and over.
